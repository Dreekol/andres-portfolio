## The problem

HealthPartners’ Specialty Pharmacy Network contracts directly with more than 25 specialty pharmacies. Tracking drugs and pricing across 12 vendors was manual, and manual meant errors. The team wanted Power Platform to reduce that error and automate the process.

## What I did

**Started with the people doing the work.** I interviewed stakeholders about their needs, pain points and expected workflows before drawing anything.

**Designed the data model and the journeys together.** Contract management and price calculation were the critical tasks, so I mapped how the data related and designed each journey to cut manual effort.

**Tested early in mid-fidelity.** Mid-fidelity designs let stakeholders react before development, catching issues while they were still cheap to fix.

**Designed for accessibility from the start**, following guidelines so every user could work in the tools.

I applied the same approach to related internal tools: a medication-optimization registry for logging calls, outcomes and hours, and a member-incentives tracker.

## Result

Tools that streamline the workflow, reduce errors and make pricing calculations more accurate. I worked closely with developers, analysts and stakeholders through implementation.
