I designed the account setup flow brokers use to bring a trucking client to Intact and quote coverage. It splits a long application into six steps, listed down the left, with Save and Exit always in reach.

**Look the client up instead of retyping them.** A lookup fills in the client’s details, and what the system already knows arrives pre-filled.

<Figure src={s2} alt="Step two of the flow with a lookup and contact fields, shown as a wireframe" caption="Step two: a lookup fills in the client’s details." />

**Ask in the broker’s words.** Each section opens with a plain-language question instead of a form label.

**Shape the quote around the client.** Product choices and the details of the client’s fleet build the quote, then the broker sets limits and benefits.
