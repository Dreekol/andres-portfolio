## The challenge

The program is Twin Cities Orthopedics’ employee wellness program. It starts with a biometric screening. Each participant gets a report with their results and recommendations, then 8 to 10 months of weekly emails, each with a call to action. Completing enough of them earns an incentive.

The program ran on Microsoft 365 and Tableau. Revo Health wanted an app that could schedule the content, track the calls to action, automate reporting and be white-labeled for other employers.

<Step n={1}>Interview</Step>

I interviewed the program’s stakeholders to understand who does what, from the screening to the incentive.

<Step n={2}>Map today</Step>

I mapped each group’s part in the current process, so everyone could see the whole program in one place.

<Step n={3}>Workshop tomorrow</Step>

In workflow workshops, the groups worked through how they expected the process to run in the new app.

<Step n={4}>Blueprint</Step>

I documented the new process in a service blueprint, the shared reference for the design.

<Step n={5}>Design</Step>

From the blueprint, I designed the app workflows, from entering a participant’s report to their tasks and a department view, and the weekly email challenges that guide employees toward healthy habits.
