## The challenge

The program is a value-based, fixed-price offering for total joint replacement. Before surgery, every patient needs an assessment to confirm they’re a good fit for outpatient care, and the care team follows each one for 90 days afterward.

Revo Health wanted software to make qualification faster, automate communication and reminders, capture patient data and outcomes, and connect with Twin Cities Orthopedics’ systems. The first release would focus on qualification, automated communication and milestone tracking, and it had to scale and meet HIPAA requirements.

<Step n={1}>Learn the program</Step>

The program brief set out what the software needed to cover: case management, pre-surgical screening, content, surveys and reminders, tracking each patient’s care, reporting, and automated forms and approvals. I used it to understand the whole program before narrowing in on the first release.

<Step n={2}>Listen to the nurses</Step>

I wrote an interview guide about the team’s challenges, how they split work before and after surgery, and what a rolled-up dashboard should show, then interviewed the nursing team’s lead.

**What we heard.** Nurses plan their day from filtered lists: assessment calls, pre-op physicals, and the calls that follow a patient from discharge to 90 days. Patients call in constantly, so nurses need to jump between several patients at once. Follow-ups lived on sticky notes and calendar reminders, because tasks in the old system didn’t alert anyone.

<Step n={3}>Follow their workflow</Step>

I reshaped the app around the nurses’ natural workflow. They start from the same filtered lists they already use, open a patient’s chart from the list, and keep several patients open at once. Each chart is organized by tabs.

<Step n={4}>Qualify faster</Step>

I prioritized the disqualifying factors in the assessment, so a patient who isn’t a fit for outpatient surgery is caught early, before the team spends time on the rest of the intake.

<Step n={5}>Remind automatically</Step>

A dashboard shows assessment calls at a glance: what’s overdue, what’s due this week and what’s coming up this month. Reminders are automated, so follow-ups no longer depend on notes and memory.
