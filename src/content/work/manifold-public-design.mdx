## Who they are

Manifold Public Design is a creative studio built for a hybrid world. Its cross-functional team of designers, artists, fabricators, researchers and technologists shapes a client's experience both online and in real life. A single project might include a new identity, a website, a campaign and the interior of a clinic.

The studio specializes in healthcare, education and other public-service organizations, and it starts from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to beautiful design, whether a project is big or small. Its values are openness, accessibility, beauty, inspiration and innovation. Every project starts with discovery, and clients are invited into the process from concept to rendering.

Its work spans four areas: digital design, brand identity and campaigns, marketing and content, and environmental design and fabrication. Recent projects include an identity and website refresh for Ellie Mental Health ahead of its national franchising, a Northwoods-inspired identity for Minnesota's first psychedelic therapy clinic, and a physical-to-digital refresh for a Saint Paul charter school.

## The website

The site had one big idea to get across: a single studio that works across digital and physical space. The homepage says it directly, “People today navigate fluidly between digital and physical spaces. Luckily, so do we.” Then it lays out the four areas of focus, each paired with a collage of the studio's work.
