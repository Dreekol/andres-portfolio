## The problem

Cargill customers needed one place to manage their business with Cargill, and a way in that didn’t depend on emailing someone for an account.

## What I did

**Partnered with the product owner and business analyst** to shape a portal that met business needs and followed design best practices.

**Made the value clear before sign-in.** The public pages explain what customers can do in myCargill, with a direct path to request access.

<Figure src={secondary} alt="myCargill public page with service highlights, shown as a wireframe" caption="The public homepage explains the portal before asking for anything." />

**Turned access into a guided, self-serve request.** Contact details, business information and the request itself are separate steps, finishing with a clear confirmation.
