## The challenge

Cargill's sales reps knew their customers well, but not always which other Cargill products those customers could use. The goal was a tool inside the sales system reps already worked in. It would point each rep to the right customers and the right products, and make it easy to act.

## What I designed

The screens below are shown as patterns: the layout and how it works, without the application's content.

**Find the right customers, then dig in.** Reps start from a ranked list of target customers. Each account opens to one view: how the latest marketing campaign landed, suggested products with how strong each opportunity is, and the customer's purchase history.

<Figure src={accounts} alt="Two wireframe patterns: a ranked list of target customers, and an account view with campaign status, suggested products and purchase history" caption="The target list and the account view, shown as patterns." />

**Act on every suggestion.** For each suggested product, a rep can create an opportunity, snooze it until later, or mark it complete. A short note keeps the next person in the loop.

<Figure src={actions} alt="Three dialog patterns: snooze a suggestion, mark it complete, and add an opportunity" caption="Snooze, mark complete, or add an opportunity." />

**Learn from every answer.** In the future state, reps tell the tool why a product wasn't a fit or how the conversation went, so the next round of suggestions gets better.

<Figure src={feedback} alt="Three feedback dialog patterns with illustrations: why a product wasn't a match, how the conversation went, and a thank-you" caption="The future state: feedback that improves the suggestions." />

## Result

The team got an MVP design for the cross-selling tool, shaped by what reps and customers told us, and a future state to grow into.
