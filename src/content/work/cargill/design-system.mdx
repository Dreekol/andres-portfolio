## The problem

Cargill teams around the world built products that looked and behaved differently. Every inconsistency cost design and development time, and customers felt the seams.

## What I did

**Helped stand up a white-label system for global adoption.** I produced the documentation, onboarded and trained new team members, created the working ceremonies, and audited global products against the system.

**Agreed a design-to-development contract.** I co-created a QA relationship with developers and a negotiation process for feasibility, so designs translated into code as intended.

**Extended the system to data.** With the global IT group, I turned complex application-health data into role-based Power BI dashboards, so every level of stakeholder could find, understand and report on what they needed. I checked in with developers often to keep the designs buildable in Power BI.

**Wrote it down so it scales.** I documented my design choices against chart-usage best practice and added components so future reports stay consistent.
