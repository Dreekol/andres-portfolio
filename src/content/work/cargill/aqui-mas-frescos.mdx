## The problem

New users of a Latin American mobile experience were struggling to register. Before redesigning anything, the team needed to know where and why.

## What I did

**Mapped the current registration flow** step by step to see where it created friction.

<Figure src={reg} alt="Current new-user registration flow mapped as a diagram" caption="The existing registration flow, mapped." />

**Synthesized findings into opportunities**, from asking for less information to letting people register with accounts they already have.

<Pair a={findings} aAlt="Findings summary" b={opps} bAlt="Opportunities: ways to reduce the pain during registration" caption="Findings, then opportunities." />

**Planned the work in phases**, with deliverables, a typical timeline and next steps.

<Pair a={deliv} aAlt="Deliverables and typical timeline" b={next} bAlt="Next steps with time estimates" caption="Deliverables and next steps." />

## Result

A shared understanding of the registration problem and a phased plan to fix it.
