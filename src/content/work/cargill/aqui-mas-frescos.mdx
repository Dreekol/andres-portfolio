## The problem

The team wanted to improve how new users register in a Latin American mobile experience, and needed a clear picture of the current flow before redesigning anything.

## What I did

**Mapped the current registration flow** step by step to see where it created friction.

<Figure src={reg} alt="Registration flow diagram with details blurred" caption="The existing registration flow, mapped." />

**Synthesized findings into opportunities** for the team to prioritize.

**Planned the work in phases**, with deliverables, a typical timeline and next steps.

## Result

A shared understanding of the registration problem and a phased plan to fix it.
