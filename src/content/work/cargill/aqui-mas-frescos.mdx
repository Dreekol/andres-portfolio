## The product

Aquí + Frescos is Cargill's food shopping brand in Honduras. People buy chicken, dairy, meat and groceries from Cargill's own brands in one place, either at neighborhood stores or through a mobile app with home delivery.

<Pair a={brand} aAlt="Aquí + Frescos ad: a delivery scooter and the app on a phone" b={onePlace} bAlt="Aquí + Frescos ad: a storefront surrounded by groceries, dairy, meat and other products" narrow caption="The brand's own marketing: home delivery, and all the shopping in one place." />

## The problem

New customers had to create an account before they could order, and sign-up and login were where they got stuck. The team needed a clear picture of the current experience before redesigning it, and a way for design to work with their product and development teams.

## What I did

**Mapped the registration flow** end to end: every screen and decision a new customer goes through before they can shop.

<Figure src={reg} alt="Registration flow diagram with details blurred" caption="The existing registration flow, mapped." />

**Assessed the experience** and grouped what I found by area, from login and password reset to the activation email. Then I turned the findings into ways to take the pain out of registration.

<Pair a={findings} aAlt="Findings slide grouped by area, details blurred" b={opps} bAlt="Opportunities slide: ways to reduce the pain during registration, details blurred" caption="Findings by area, then opportunities for registration." />

**Set up how design would work with the team.** I defined a work cadence that takes a ticket through research, concepts, team validation, prototyping, stakeholder validation and dev handoff with QA. I also organized the Figma file by stage, so everyone could see what was in exploration, up for review, waiting on approval or ready for development.

<Pair a={ways} aAlt="Ways of working slide: the work cadence from project onboarding to development" b={deliv} bAlt="Deliverables slide: Figma file stages and a typical timeline" caption="The work cadence, and how the design file was organized." />

**Prioritized what comes next:** the app enhancements in order of priority, and a short plan that started with a component library and the registration and onboarding fixes.

<Pair a={future} aAlt="Future enhancement prioritization slide, descriptions blurred" b={next} bAlt="Next steps slide, timings blurred" caption="Enhancements in priority order, and the next steps." />

## Result

The team came away with a shared picture of where sign-up broke down, a working rhythm for design and development, and a prioritized plan to fix registration first.
