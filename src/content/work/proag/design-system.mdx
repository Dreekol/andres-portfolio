## The problem

The UX strategy pointed to one cohesive experience across ProAg’s tools, and design and development needed a shared foundation to build it on.

<Step n={1}>Learn the business</Step>

I studied ProAg’s systems and information architecture, and how its teams work.

<Step n={2}>Plan</Step>

I ran planning workshops to decide what to tackle first, and interviewed stakeholders about how they work with design.

<Step n={3}>Pitch options</Step>

I presented different design-system approaches, not a single answer, so IT and product management could weigh expectations and feasibility before committing.

<Step n={4}>Set principles</Step>

I set four principles the system answers to: purposeful, versatile, compelling and thoughtful.

<Figure src={principles} alt="Design system principles: purposeful, versatile, compelling and thoughtful, each with a short description" caption="Four principles for the system." />

<Step n={5}>Build foundations</Step>

The foundations cover color with accessible tints and shades, Proxima Nova in three weights, and icons built on the Carbon set, extended with agriculture-specific ones.

<Figure src={palette} alt="Color palette: ProAg greens, blues, reds, oranges, lime and neutrals, each with tints and shades" caption="Color, with accessible tints and shades." />

<Figure src={type} alt="Typography specimen for Proxima Nova in regular, semibold and bold" caption="Type: Proxima Nova in three weights." />

<Figure src={icons} alt="Icon grid built on the Carbon icon set, with agriculture-specific icons" caption="Iconography on the Carbon set." />

<Step n={6}>Put it to work</Step>

I organized ProAg’s Figma instance around the components and created the documentation, ceremonies, templates and onboarding material for people joining. Defining components in shared chunks of information gave design and development one vocabulary, keeping components and products consistent.

<ProtectedNote href="/private/work/proag/design-system" title="Design system library and specs" text="The library, component specs, the working cadence and code handoff." />

## Result

ProAg teams came away with one documented system: principles, foundations, a working cadence and onboarding. The quoting concepts were among the first work to apply it.
