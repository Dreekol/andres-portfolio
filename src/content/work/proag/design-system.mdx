## The problem

The UX strategy pointed to one cohesive experience across ProAg’s tools, and design and development needed a shared foundation to build it on.

## What I did

**Learned the business first.** I studied ProAg’s systems and information architecture, and how its teams work.

**Ran planning workshops to decide what to tackle first**, and interviewed stakeholders about how they work with design.

**Pitched options, not a single answer.** I presented different design-system approaches so IT and product management could weigh expectations and feasibility before committing.

## How teams use it

**Built the operating model around the components.** I organized ProAg’s Figma instance and created the documentation, ceremonies, templates and onboarding material for people joining.

**Gave design and development one vocabulary.** Defining components in shared chunks of information created a language between teams, keeping components and products consistent.

## Result

ProAg teams came away with one documented system: principles, foundations, a working cadence and onboarding. The quoting concepts were among the first work to apply it.
