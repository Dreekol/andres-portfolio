## The problem

ProAg wanted a more cohesive experience across the tools its agents use every day, and needed to know where to start. Our SDG team ran a two-phase UX strategy project to find out.

## Phase 1: discovery

**Listened first.** We interviewed ProAg stakeholders across roles and agents across regions, then affinity-mapped the recordings into themes.

**Toured the systems.** System demos gave us the context around the tools agents rely on.

**Ideated together.** In “How might we” workshops with ProAg, small teams generated ideas and plotted the strongest themes on an impact and effort matrix. Co-concepting sessions turned them into statements written from the agent’s point of view.

<Figure src={coconcept} alt="Co-concepting session slide with the board and notes blurred" caption="Co-concepting, from the agent’s point of view." />

**Framed the choice.** We laid out strategic paths for leadership to weigh against the research and the product vision.

## Phase 2: a plan for the plan

**Gave the team a shared picture of the agent.** We wrote an agent archetype and ran a survey to test what we had heard at a larger scale.

**Concepted a starting point.** We sketched early concepts for where agents begin their work.

**Looked for quick wins.** We identified small improvements that could ship ahead of larger changes.

**Turned it into a roadmap.** Strategy sessions with leaders across disciplines produced a phased horizon map for product strategy, user research and design.
