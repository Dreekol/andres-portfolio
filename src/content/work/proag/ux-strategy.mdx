## The problem

ProAg wanted a more cohesive experience across the tools its agents use every day, and needed to know where to start. Our SDG team ran a two-phase UX strategy project to find out.

## Phase 1: discovery

<Step n={1}>Listen</Step>

We interviewed ProAg stakeholders across roles and agents across regions, then affinity-mapped the recordings into themes. System demos gave us the context around the tools agents rely on.

<Step n={2}>Ideate together</Step>

In “How might we” workshops with ProAg, small teams generated ideas and plotted the strongest themes on an impact and effort matrix. Co-concepting sessions turned them into statements written from the agent’s point of view.

<Figure src={coconcept} alt="Co-concepting session slide with the board and notes blurred" caption="Co-concepting, from the agent’s point of view." />

<Step n={3}>Frame the choice</Step>

We laid out strategic paths for leadership to weigh against the research and the product vision.

## Phase 2: a plan for the plan

<Step n={4}>Picture the agent</Step>

We wrote an agent archetype to give the team a shared picture of the agent, and ran a survey to test what we had heard at a larger scale.

<Step n={5}>Find a start</Step>

We sketched early concepts for where agents begin their work, and identified small improvements that could ship ahead of larger changes.

<Step n={6}>Plan</Step>

Strategy sessions with leaders across disciplines produced a phased horizon map for product strategy, user research and design.
