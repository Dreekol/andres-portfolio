<Step n={1}>Heuristic evaluation</Step>

We walked through the quoting flow and graded it against Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics, scoring each issue by how often it happens, how hard it is to overcome and how long it persists.

<Figure src={heuristics} alt="Illustrations of Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics" caption="The 10 heuristics we evaluated against." />

<Figure src={severity} alt="Severity scale from 0, no violations, to 4, usability catastrophe" caption="How each issue was scored." />

<Step n={2}>Quoter concepts</Step>

Building on earlier concepts, we made a low to mid-fidelity prototype, applied the design system, and reviewed it with agents and stakeholders.

<Step n={3}>Printout validation</Step>

We did the same for printed summaries.

## What came out of it

A prioritized list of usability issues, tested concepts and clear next steps for the quoting experience.
