## Heuristic evaluation

We walked through the “create a quote” flow and graded it against Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics, scoring each issue by how often it happens, how hard it is to overcome and how long it persists.

<Figure src={heuristics} alt="Illustrations of Jakob Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics" caption="The 10 heuristics we evaluated against." />

<Figure src={severity} alt="Severity scale from 0, no violations, to 4, usability catastrophe" caption="How each issue was scored." />

<Figure src={summary} alt="Evaluation summary: 38 items, including 16 major problems and 1 catastrophe" caption="38 issues documented, 16 of them major." />

## Quoter and printout concepts

Building on earlier concepts, we made a low to mid-fidelity prototype with two landing-page variants and a customer detail page, applied the design system, and reviewed it with 3 agents and 3 stakeholders. We did the same for redesigned printout summaries.

## What we learned

Agents responded well to the cleaner look and consolidated navigation. Where feedback split, it split by how each agent works, which showed ProAg needed more than one agent persona before going further.
