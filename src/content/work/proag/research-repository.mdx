Research lived in separate files from project to project. Just as the design system holds the design practice together, a repository keeps research usable after each project ends.

<Figure src={foundation} alt="Illustration: everyone benefits from a research repository, with researchers, sales, support and product teams contributing and drawing from it" caption="The case for a repository." />

**Defined the requirements.** What a shared repository needed to do for the teams contributing to it and using it.

**Compared tools and recommended one.** We evaluated options against those requirements and made a recommendation.

**Showed how it would work.** A demo walked through contributing, tagging and analyzing research.
