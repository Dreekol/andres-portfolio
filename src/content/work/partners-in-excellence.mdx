With stakeholders, I assessed the current state and designed a future-state framework for the dashboard.

<Step n={2}>Design the cascade</Step>

I architected a “cascade of granularity”: five role-based views that run from the whole organization down to individual teams, aligned with row-level security so each person sees what their role allows.
