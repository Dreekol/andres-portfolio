With cross-functional stakeholders, I assessed the current state and designed a future-state framework for one reporting dashboard spanning clinical, financial and HR systems. I architected a “cascade of granularity”: five role-based views that run from the whole organization down to individual teams, aligned with row-level security, and set chart guidelines for desktop and mobile.
