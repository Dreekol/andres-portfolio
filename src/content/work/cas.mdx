## Who they are

Component Assembly Systems (CAS) has been building since 1964, when it was founded as Score Carpentry. Today CAS and its subsidiary, Component West, are among the premier drywall and acoustical ceiling contractors in the United States, with regional offices from New York and Washington, D.C. to Las Vegas and California.

CAS has completed $6 billion of work in every major building sector. Its portfolio includes One World Trade Center, the National Museum of African American History and Culture, Yankee Stadium and JFK's new Terminal 1. Its services have grown from framing and drywall to specialty ceilings, millwork, exterior wall systems and pre-construction planning. Along the way, it has invested in safety training beyond OSHA standards, building information modeling, its own project information platform, and a teaming program that has brought small and minority businesses into more than $130 million of its work.

## What the site needed to do

The CAS website was due for modernization. The new one had to show owners, general contractors and design teams what CAS builds and how well, explain its six services, and point people to the right regional office or to a career with the company. It also had to be something the team could keep current on their own.

I led the redesign. I evaluated the existing site, set goals and success metrics, and built the roadmap. I worked with CAS stakeholders through exercises, workshops and co-working sessions to organize the content and settle the layout and style together. From there I created the wireframes, led design critiques with junior designers, and planned how content would move to the new site. I chose technology that would keep the site accessible and easy for CAS to update, then tested iteratively with users.
