## The problem

Annuities are usually sold through an agent. American National wanted buyers to be able to purchase a fixed annuity entirely online, which meant the site had to do the agent’s job: explain a financial product plainly, show what it would earn, and carry a legally heavy application without losing people.

## What I designed

**A landing page that answers “is this for me?” before asking for anything.** It leads with the guaranteed rate, compares the product against CDs and savings in one table, and breaks the process into three plain steps.

<Figure src={landing} alt="Full American National MYGA landing page: hero, rate callout, comparison table, ratings, three-step process and FAQs" caption="The landing page, top to bottom. Live at annuities.americannational.com." tall />

**A calculator that shows the money first.** Buyers enter a premium and pick a 3, 5 or 7-year period, and the result shows future value, base rate and interest together, with a chart of growth year by year. Applying comes after the number makes sense.

<Figure src={calc} alt="Rate calculator with premium amount, accumulation period options, a growth chart and future account value of $65,193" caption="Sample estimate: $50,000 over five years." />

**An application that sets expectations before it asks.** The intro lists everything a buyer will need, and the required disclosures are presented in a readable panel before consent.

<Pair a={intro} aAlt="Application intro listing what applicants need before they begin" b={terms} bAlt="Terms and conditions page with electronic signature and customer notice disclosures" caption="What to expect, then the disclosures, before any personal details." />

**Designed for phones as much as desktops.**

## Result

The flow is live, and anyone can buy American National’s MYG Annuity online from start to finish.
