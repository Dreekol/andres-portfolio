For EXCEL, a program pairing surgical care with a recovery retreat, I reshaped the app around nurses’ natural workflow, prioritized disqualifying factors to speed up patient qualification, and automated reminders.

<Figure src={excel} alt="EXCEL care dashboard, shown as a wireframe" caption="EXCEL: tracking each patient through the program." />

For Habits to Health, I interviewed stakeholders, mapped each group’s part in the current process, and ran workflow workshops on how they expected it to work. I documented the new process in a service blueprint, then designed workflows and weekly email challenges that guide employees toward healthy habits.
