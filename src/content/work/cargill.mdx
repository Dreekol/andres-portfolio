## The problem

Cargill is one of the largest privately owned companies in the world, and its digital products looked and behaved differently from team to team. Every inconsistency cost design and development time, and customers felt the seams.

## What I did

**Helped stand up a white-label design system for global adoption.** I produced the documentation, onboarded and trained new team members, created the working ceremonies, and audited global products against the system.

**Designed the myCargill customer portal.** I partnered with the product owner and business analyst so the portal met business needs while following design best practices, including a self-serve access request that walks customers through it step by step.

**Agreed a design-to-development contract.** I co-created a QA relationship with developers and a negotiation process for feasibility, so designs translated into code as intended.

**Made complex data readable.** With the global IT group, I turned application-health data into role-based Power BI dashboards, so each level of stakeholder could find and report on what they needed. I documented chart-usage standards so future reports would stay consistent.

<Pair a={vstyle} aAlt="Data visualization style guidelines" b={vusage} bAlt="Data visualization usage guidelines" caption="Data-viz guidance in the design system, adapted from established best practice." />

**Ran research for new markets.** For a Latin American mobile experience, I synthesized findings into opportunities and next steps.

<Figure src={amf} alt="Research findings summary slide for a Latin American mobile experience" caption="Research synthesis, anonymized." />

## Result

A shared system, documentation and process that Cargill teams use to build consistently, plus a company-wide standard for gathering feedback from its users.
