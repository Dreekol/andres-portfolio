<Step n={1}>Start from home</Step>

The home is built around four jobs: calls, reports, record hours and other payors. Payor choices moved out of a popup and into the main navigation, so nothing is hidden.

<Figure src={home} alt="Registry home with four entry points, shown as a wireframe" caption="Registry home." />

<Step n={2}>See who’s due</Step>

Call invites surface follow-ups and this week’s invites at the top, with filters by program and outcome.

<Step n={3}>Log the call</Step>

Logging a call captures the member, call details and outcome in one form.

<Step n={4}>Report</Step>

Reporting and time tracking live in the same place as the outreach.
