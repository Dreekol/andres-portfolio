## The problem

Contracting and pricing for HealthPartners’ specialty pharmacy network was tracked by hand. The team wanted a tool that would reduce errors and automate the process.

## What I did

**Started with the people doing the work.** I interviewed stakeholders about their needs, pain points and expected workflows before drawing anything.

**Designed the data model and the journeys together.** Contract management and price calculation were the critical tasks, so I mapped how the data related and designed each journey to cut manual effort.

<Figure src={pricing} alt="Pricing screen within a contract, shown as a wireframe" caption="Pricing lives inside the contract it belongs to." />

**Tested early in mid-fidelity**, so stakeholders could react before development, while issues were still cheap to fix.

**Designed for accessibility from the start**, following guidelines so every user could work in the tool.

<ProtectedNote href="/private/work/healthpartners/pharmacy-contracting" title="Pharmacy contracting screens" text="Contract detail and fee schedules." />

## Result

One tool replaced the manual tracking of contracts, fee schedules and pricing. I worked closely with developers, analysts and stakeholders through implementation.
