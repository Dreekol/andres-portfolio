## The problem

Contracting and pricing for a HealthPartners pharmacy network was tracked by hand. The team wanted a tool that would reduce errors and automate the process.

## How the work ran

<Process label="How the work ran" steps={[
  { title: 'Interview', text: 'Start with the people doing the work.' },
  { title: 'Model and map', text: 'Design the data model and the journeys together.' },
  { title: 'Test early', text: 'Put mid-fidelity designs in front of stakeholders.' },
  { title: 'Build it in', text: 'Accessibility from the start, then support through implementation.' },
]} />

<Step n={1}>Interview</Step>

I interviewed stakeholders about their needs, pain points and expected workflows before drawing anything.

<Step n={2}>Model and map</Step>

Contract management and price calculation were the critical tasks, so I mapped how the data related and designed each journey to cut manual effort.

<Figure src={pricing} alt="Pricing screen within a contract, shown as a wireframe" caption="Pricing lives inside the contract it belongs to." />

<Step n={3}>Test early</Step>

I tested in mid-fidelity, so stakeholders could react before development, while issues were still cheap to fix.

<Step n={4}>Build it in</Step>

I designed for accessibility from the start, following guidelines so every user could work in the tool, and worked closely with developers, analysts and stakeholders through implementation.

<ProtectedNote href="/private/work/healthpartners/contract-management" title="Contract management screens" text="Contract detail and fee schedules." />

## Result

One tool replaced the manual tracking of contracts, fee schedules and pricing.
