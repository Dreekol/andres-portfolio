## The challenge

The website is how Harris County Public Health reaches the 4.7 million people it serves. Every division’s campaign sent residents there to learn more, so the site had to keep up with all of them.

<Step n={1}>Keep it current</Step>

I maintained and updated the department’s website together with the public information officer in charge of each division. It was also the destination for the campaigns I designed for them.

<Step n={2}>Post urgent updates</Step>

When there was a public health risk, I posted the important messaging residents needed, such as emergency updates.

<Step n={3}>Design the blog</Step>

I designed a blog system for the site: a responsive layout for articles by the department’s leaders and experts, on topics like Hurricane Harvey one year later, the peak of hurricane season and public health’s role in the community. Each article carries its author’s name and role, and the layout works from phone to desktop.
