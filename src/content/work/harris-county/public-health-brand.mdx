<Step n={1}>Logo</Step>

I developed the Harris County Public Health logo.

<Step n={2}>Brand standards</Step>

I established the brand standards used throughout the organization.

<Step n={3}>Harris County Pets</Step>

I also established the brand for Harris County Pets, the county’s animal shelter, so it reads as part of the same family as Public Health.

<Figure src={family} alt="The Harris County Public Health and Harris County Pets logos side by side" caption="Harris County Pets shares the Public Health palette of blue, green, pink and yellow." />

<Figure src={pets} alt="The countypets.com homepage with the logo, navigation and color-coded Adopt, Foster, Volunteer and Calendar panels" caption="The Pets brand on countypets.com today." />

<Step n={4}>Carry it everywhere</Step>

The standards carried into the department’s own displays and publications, like a lit exhibit built from the brand’s color bands. I also led the design of “Harris Cares 2020: A Vision of Health in Harris County,” the department’s report.

<Pair a={exhibit} aAlt="A lit exhibit: a backdrop of blue, purple and pink bands reading “Building a Healthy Community” behind an orange and green counter with the Harris County Public Health logo" b={report} bAlt="The Harris Cares 2020 report, a navy cover with a large yellow 2020 and the Public Health logo" caption="An exhibit and the Harris Cares 2020 report." />

They also shaped the campaigns, events, program brands and vehicle wraps I designed for the department’s divisions, shown in [the division campaigns and events case study](/harris-county/division-campaigns).
